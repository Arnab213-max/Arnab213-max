### A LITTLE INTRODUCTION

Hey, I'm **Arnab Maharjan**, a BCA student from Nepal.

I'm someone who enjoys exploring different things. Technology got me interested in building things, design got me interested in how things look and feel, and life outside the screen keeps me curious about everything else.

I spend my time learning, experimenting, creating and occasionally figuring things out the hard way.

I'm not trying to stick to one particular identity. I like having different interests and seeing where they take me.
